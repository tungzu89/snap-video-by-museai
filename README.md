## English

This package contains the `snap_video_by_monz` skill: the complete build
specification for the "Snap video by Monz" downloader — paste a link and get the
original, watermark-free video, with quality options up to 4K, delivered as an
iPhone-ready MP4 (H.264 + AAC) that saves straight to the Photos library.
The backend runs on yt-dlp + ffmpeg, capped at 80MB per video,
public videos only.

### How to use

1. Just send your Muse the link to this repo — no file download needed
   (GitHub's mobile site doesn't even show a ZIP download button).
2. Say: "Read the skill in this link and build Snap video by Monz for me."
3. Your Muse builds a dedicated Space inside your own account, verifies it
   against a real public link, and hands it over ready to use.

### Notes

- Your copy starts with an empty history and runs under your own Muse account.
- Short clips (TikTok/Douyin style) work best; long videos at high quality
  may need a lower quality setting.
